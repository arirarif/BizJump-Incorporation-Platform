Refund Policy

We stand behind our work. Here's exactly what you're entitled to.

Last Updated: April 18, 2026

💡 Simple summary: If we haven't started filing your documents yet, you can get a full refund of our service fee. State government fees are never refundable once submitted.

1. Our Service Fee — Full Refund Window

You are eligible for a full refund of BizJump's service fee if you request it within 24 hours of placing your order and your documents have not yet been submitted to the state.

To request a refund, contact us at support@bizjump.com with your order number.

2. State Filing Fees — Non-Refundable

State government filing fees are paid directly to the state on your behalf. Once your documents have been submitted to the state, these fees cannot be refunded under any circumstances. This applies regardless of whether the state approves or rejects your filing.

3. After Filing Has Started — No Refund

Once we have submitted your formation documents to the state agency, our service fee is non-refundable. This is because our work has been completed at that point.

4. BizJump Errors

If BizJump makes an error in your filing that is our fault (not caused by incorrect information you provided), we will:

  • Correct the error at no additional charge, or
  • Issue a full refund of our service fee if the error cannot be corrected

We are not responsible for errors resulting from incorrect information submitted by you.

5. Registered Agent and Subscription Services

Registered Agent service and other annual subscriptions may be cancelled at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. We do not provide prorated refunds for partial subscription periods.

6. Add-On Services

Add-on services (EIN filing, Operating Agreement, etc.) are refundable only if work has not yet begun. Once we have started preparing your document, no refund is available for that add-on.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please:

  • Email support@bizjump.com with subject line "Refund Request — Order #XXXXX"
  • Include your order number and reason for the refund request
  • We will respond within 1–2 business days

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our refund policy, please contact us or email support@bizjump.com.
