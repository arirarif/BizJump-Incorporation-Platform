> - Subscription Services — Registered Agent, website hosting, compliance monitoring
> - WooCommerce Checkout — Stripe, PayPal, ACH
> - Client Dashboard — view order status, upload documents, track progress, download deliverables
> - Admin Order Management — view formation details, track services, update status, manage subscriptions
>
> **Technology Stack:**
